## 文字说明（拟）
Majority Rules is a daily dilemma game built with Devvit Web. Every day,
every player gets the same dilemma — two everyday choices, one question:
what would most people pick? Pick a side, see the split, and build a
streak when you side with the majority. One screen, two buttons, no setup.
The result card is made to be copied into the comments, so the argument
continues where Reddit lives. Thirty dilemmas rotate on a date seed, with
a practice mode for extra rounds.
